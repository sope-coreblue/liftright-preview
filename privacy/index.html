Privacy

What we collect

When you request a quote or contact us we collect the details you provide: your name, company, contact details, equipment information and anything you choose to tell us about your site and requirements.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, confirm scope, prepare a quote and, if you become a customer, to schedule and record inspections. We do not sell your information.

Inspection records

Records of inspections we perform are retained so they can be referenced at later inspections and support traceability. They are not published or shared without your authorisation, except where required by law.

Analytics

We use privacy-conscious analytics to understand which pages are useful. The contents of enquiry forms are never sent to analytics.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep. Contact us using the details on this site.
