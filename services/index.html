Terms that overlap

Equipment names vary by industry and supplier. A few plain definitions:

Inspection
A competent person examines the equipment and documents its condition, disposition and next actions. This is our core service.
Operator training or certification
Training and evaluating the people who use the equipment. We do not provide this.
Dielectric testing
Electrical testing of insulating components on aerial devices. A distinct service, offered separately where available.
Non-destructive testing (NDT)
Specialist methods such as magnetic particle or ultrasonic testing to find hidden cracks. Not included in a standard inspection unless agreed.
Engineering review
Assessment by a licensed professional engineer, for example after a modification or major repair. Arranged separately where required.
Repair
Corrective work. Always a separate decision and a separate authorisation from the inspection.
