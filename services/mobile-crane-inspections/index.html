Mobile crane and boom truck inspections

Inspections of boom trucks and mobile cranes within our accepted configurations, carried out at your yard or site with a documented record for each unit. We state clearly what is in scope, what is excluded and what the next step is.

Not every crane is covered. We inspect the configurations listed below. Overhead cranes, tower cranes and other lifting systems are outside this service.

Equipment we inspect

  • Telescopic boom trucks within the accepted capacity range
  • Articulating (knuckle-boom) truck-mounted cranes within the accepted capacity range
  • Other mobile crane types by prior agreement only

Send the manufacturer, model, rated capacity and configuration. We confirm eligibility before quoting. Capacity limits and accepted types are listed once approved.

What the inspection covers

  • Identification: manufacturer, model, serial, load charts, data plates and decals present and legible
  • Boom sections, pins, visible welds, wear pads and sheaves
  • Wire rope, hook, hook block and rigging hardware for wear, damage and security
  • Outriggers, stabilisers, pads and mounting to the chassis
  • Hydraulic system: cylinders, hoses, fittings, holding valves and leaks
  • Controls, limit devices, load moment or capacity indicators and anti-two-block devices as fitted, checked for function
  • Visible condition of the subframe, turret and rotation components

The inspection is visual and functional against the manufacturer's requirements and the agreed scope. Load testing and non-destructive testing are separate services and are not implied.

What is not included

  • Overhead cranes, gantry cranes, tower cranes and hoists
  • Cranes outside the accepted configurations or capacity range
  • Load testing, non-destructive testing or engineering assessment unless specifically agreed in the scope
  • Operator training or certification
  • Commercial vehicle safety inspections of the chassis
  • Repairs or adjustments. Corrective work needs your separate authorisation

Preparing for the visit

Where the work happens

At your yard or site. We need firm, level ground to set up on outriggers, clear space and height to extend the boom, and a site contact who can authorise operation.

  • Level set-up area with room for outriggers and boom extension
  • Unit fuelled and ready with load charts on board
  • Access and any site induction arranged

How to prepare

  • Send the manufacturer, model, serial, rated capacity and chassis unit number
  • Confirm the load charts and manuals are on board
  • Gather previous inspection and wire rope records if available
  • Clear a level set-up area
  • Confirm site rules, induction and parking

Records we ask for

  • Data plate and load chart information
  • Previous inspection records, if any
  • Wire rope replacement and repair or modification records, if any

Records and follow-up

During the visit

  1. We confirm the unit list and scope with your site contact
  2. Each crane is identified and matched to its records
  3. A documented walkaround records condition, including serviceable items, with photographs
  4. Functional checks of controls, limit devices and indicators are carried out in a safe set-up area
  5. Findings are reviewed with your site contact before we leave

What you receive

  • A record for each unit stating the scope performed, identification, findings with photographs, disposition and next actions
  • A fleet summary for multi-unit visits
  • Clear notes on any item that needs a specialist test or engineering review

If we find a defect

We document the finding, explain its significance and state whether the crane should be taken out of service. Corrective work is a separate decision that you authorise, whether LiftRight, a partner or your own contractor carries it out. Reinspection of the corrected items closes the record.

  • Disposition recorded per unit
  • Repair quotations, if requested, are separate from findings
  • Reinspection closes the record

Pricing and questions

What affects the price

  • Number and type of cranes
  • Capacity and configuration
  • Site location and travel
  • Set-up and scheduling
  • Whether previous records exist

Questions we are asked

Do you inspect overhead cranes?

Not under this service. We inspect mobile cranes and boom trucks within our accepted configurations. Overhead cranes and hoists are a different category.

Is a load test included?

No. The inspection is visual and functional. Where a load test is required it is a separate, specifically agreed service.

Can you inspect the wire rope?

Wire rope condition is part of the inspection. Replacement is corrective work that you authorise separately.

What happens if the crane fails?

We document the finding and the disposition, explain what it means and set out the next step. Corrective work is a separate decision, and we reinspect once it is done.

Terms used on this page

Boom truck
A telescopic or articulating crane mounted on a commercial truck chassis.
Mobile crane
A self-propelled crane on a wheeled or crawler carrier. We accept specific types and capacities.
Configuration
The crane as set up: boom length, jib, counterweight and outrigger position. Capacity depends on it.
