What is in a good inspection record?

The sections you should expect and why each one matters later.

Identification

Make, model, serial number, hour meter and the data plate details. If the record cannot be tied to one specific machine it is not much use.

Agreed scope

What was inspected and to what standard. An annual inspection, a structural inspection and a dielectric test are different scopes and the record should say which one was done.

Findings, with photographs

What was observed, including items found serviceable, not just defects. Photographs make findings checkable and help the next inspection compare condition.

Disposition and next actions

Whether the unit can stay in service, what must be corrected and by when, and what should be monitored. Corrective work is a separate decision and the record should keep it separate.

Sign-off

Who performed the inspection, who reviewed it and when. This is what gives the record its weight if a question is raised later.

Tell us about your equipment

Equipment types, site location, number of units and when you need them inspected. We confirm the scope and reply with a quote.
