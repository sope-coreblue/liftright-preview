How to prepare your site for a lifting equipment inspection

A short checklist that makes the inspection day faster and the records better.

Before the visit

Send a list of the units you want inspected with make, model and serial number. Photos of data plates are enough. Tell us about attachments, units that are out of service and anything that has been repaired or involved in an incident since the last inspection.

Records

Previous inspection records, repair invoices and modification records help us compare condition over time and confirm the right scope. Missing records do not stop an inspection.

On the day

Have the units charged or fuelled, keys available and a clear, level area where each one can be operated through its range. Arrange site access and any induction in advance, and name a site contact who can authorise operation and receive the findings.

After the visit

Review the records with us. Items that need attention now are separated from items to monitor. Any corrective work is your decision and is authorised separately.
