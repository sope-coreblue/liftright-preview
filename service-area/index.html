Site logistics

  • We come to your site, yard or project. There is no need to transport equipment to us
  • Travel is quoted by location so the cost is known before we book
  • Multi-unit sites and coordinated fleet visits make travel more efficient and the quote reflects that
  • Site access, induction and parking are agreed in advance

What we inspect

  • Forklift and lift truck inspections
  • Scissor lift, boom lift and MEWP inspections
  • Bucket truck and vehicle-mounted aerial device inspections
  • Mobile crane and boom truck inspections

Questions about coverage

Do you charge for travel?

Travel is quoted by location and shown in the quote before anything is booked. Sites with several units, or visits coordinated with nearby sites, spread that cost.

How far do you travel?

North to Collingwood and Orillia, east to Cobourg and west to Woodstock, with Niagara to the south. Sites beyond that are quoted on request, depending on the equipment and the number of units.

Can you cover several sites in different cities?

Yes. Send the list of sites and equipment and we will plan the visits and tell you what each one requires.

Not sure if we cover your location?

Give us the town or postal code and we will confirm coverage and travel.
