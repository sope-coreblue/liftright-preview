What you receive

Each unit gets its own record. It identifies the machine, states the scope that was inspected and sets out what was observed, with photographs of the items that matter.

Findings are sorted into what must be corrected before the unit returns to service, what should be monitored and what was found in good condition. The record closes with the disposition and the follow-up required.

Any repair work is quoted and authorised separately. The inspection record stays independent of any repair quotation.

Arranging an inspection

  1. Before the visit

    Send the equipment types, site location, number of units and timing. We confirm what we can inspect, agree the scope and access arrangements, and send a quote.

  2. Inspection and records

    Qualified personnel inspect each unit on site, photograph the relevant items and review the findings with your site contact before leaving. You receive a record for every unit.

  3. If follow-up is needed

    Where a defect must be corrected, you decide who does the work. Reinspection of the corrected items closes the record.
