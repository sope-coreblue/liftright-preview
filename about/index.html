About LiftRight

LiftRight Equipment inspects lifting equipment on customers' sites in Ontario and records the condition of each unit.

How we work

LiftRight inspects lifting equipment where it operates and records the condition of each unit with photographs, findings and the follow-up required.

Before quoting, we confirm the equipment, the scope that applies to it and the access needed on site. The quote describes exactly what will be inspected.

Findings are recorded per unit. Where a defect needs correction, the repair is quoted and authorised separately, whether LiftRight, a partner or your own contractor carries it out, and the record is closed after reinspection.

Some equipment falls outside what we can inspect well. When that is the case we say so.

Who performs and reviews the work

Inspections are performed by appropriately qualified personnel and recorded for each unit. Ask who will attend your site when we scope the work.
