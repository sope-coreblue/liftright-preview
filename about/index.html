About LiftRight

Clear inspections. Practical next steps.

How we work

LiftRight was started by two people who kept seeing the same gap: fleet owners with mixed equipment, inspections scattered across providers, and reports that did not tell anyone what to do next.

We organise the inspection of multiple units at a site, document each one properly, explain what the findings mean and set out the next step. Where corrective work is needed, that is a separate decision you make with clear information in front of you.

We are precise about scope. We tell you what we inspect, what we do not, who does the work and what you will receive. If a piece of equipment falls outside what we can do well, we say so.
