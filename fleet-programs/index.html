Fleet inspection programs

When a site runs forklifts, MEWPs, aerial devices or cranes from different suppliers, inspections tend to arrive at different times in different formats. A fleet program brings the equipment, the dates and the records together.

How coordination works

Send your equipment list and site details. We confirm which inspections can be coordinated into planned visits and what each one requires, including any that need a separate scope or a specialist.

Visits are scheduled with you ahead of time, around shifts and site access. Where several units of the same type are due together, one visit covers them.

How records are organised

Each unit has a line in an equipment register and its own inspection records. The register shows what was inspected, when, the outcome and when the next inspection is due.

How scope and pricing are agreed

Each program is quoted from your fleet list: equipment types, unit counts, sites and the inspection scope that applies to each. Travel is included in the quote by location.

The arrangement is reviewed with you as equipment is added or retired. There is no subscription; you pay for the inspections carried out.

How records are provided

You receive each unit record after its inspection and an updated register after each visit. Records are retained so later inspections and any separately authorised repair work can refer to them.

This is a service arrangement with shared records, not a software portal. If you already keep an equipment register, we work with your format.
