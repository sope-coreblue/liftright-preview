Fleet inspection programs

Mixed fleets usually mean several inspection providers, several formats and nobody with the whole picture. A fleet program puts the equipment, the dates and the records in one place.

What a coordinated program gives you

Coordinated visits

Forklifts, MEWPs, aerial devices and cranes at one site inspected in one planned visit where the scope allows, reducing disruption and travel.

An equipment register

Each unit's identification, inspection history, findings and disposition kept together, so the next inspection starts from a known baseline.

Due-date visibility

You can see what is due and when. We confirm scheduling with you ahead of time.

Consistent records

The same record structure for every unit and every visit. Easier to review, easier to hand to an auditor or a buyer.

History that works for you

Previous findings help scope later inspections and any separately authorised corrective work, and support traceability if an issue is ever disputed.

What this is, and is not

  • It is a coordinated inspection arrangement with disciplined records. It is not a software platform or customer portal.
  • Due-date visibility is operational hygiene. We do not claim it is unique, and we do not promise a reduction in downtime.
  • Pricing is quoted per program based on your fleet, sites and scope. There is no subscription price.
