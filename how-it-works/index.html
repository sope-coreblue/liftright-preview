How it works

What to expect before, during and after your inspection.

Before the visit

Tell us the equipment, your site location, the number of units and your timing. A unit list with make, model and serial number, or photographs of the data plates, is ideal but not needed to start.

We review eligibility, confirm the inspection scope that applies to each equipment type and send a quote that describes exactly what will be inspected. Access, site induction and parking are agreed before the visit.

Inspection and records

Appropriately qualified personnel carry out the agreed inspection or testing at your site. Each unit is identified and matched to its records, inspected through a documented walkaround with photographs, and put through functional checks in a safe area.

Findings are reviewed with your site contact before we leave. You then receive a record for each unit: identification, the scope performed, findings with photographs, the disposition and any follow-up required.

When follow-up is needed

Most inspections end with the record. Where a finding needs correction, the record says what must be done before the unit returns to service and what can be scheduled.

Any repair work is quoted and authorised separately. You choose whether LiftRight, a partner or your own contractor carries it out. Reinspection or retesting of the corrected items closes the record, and the final record documents the outcome.

The inspection record

An illustrative layout of the information each record contains. Exact fields, scope wording and sign-off follow the approved inspection procedure for each equipment type.

What the record does not do

  • It is not a guarantee of future safe operation. Condition changes with use, and pre-use checks remain the operator's responsibility.
  • It is not a repair quotation. Corrective work is scoped and authorised separately.
  • It is not a substitute for required engineering review, specialist testing or manufacturer requirements where those apply.
  • Photographs support the inspection. They do not replace it.

Common questions

How quickly will I get the records?

We agree the delivery timing with you when we scope the work and confirm it on the day.

Can you send records to my customer or head office?

Yes, with your authorisation. Tell us who should receive what.

Do you keep a copy?

We retain inspection records so they can be referenced at the next inspection and support traceability if a question is raised later. Records are not published or shared without your authorisation.

What if I disagree with a finding?

Tell us. Findings are documented with photographs so they can be reviewed, and we will discuss them with you.

Tell us about your equipment

Equipment types, site location, number of units and when you need them inspected. We confirm the scope and reply with a quote.
