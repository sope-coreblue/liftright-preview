How it works and what you receive

The whole journey, in the order it happens. No surprises at the site and no ambiguity in the record.

  1. 01

    Share your equipment details

    Tell us the equipment, your site location, the number of units and your timing. A unit list with make, model and serial, or data-plate photos, is ideal but not required to start.

  2. 02

    We confirm scope and quote

    We review eligibility, clarify the inspection scope for each equipment type, supply a quote and agree access and site arrangements with you.

  3. 03

    Qualified personnel inspect

    Appropriately qualified personnel perform the agreed inspection or testing at your site, with a documented walkaround and photographs.

  4. 04

    You receive findings and disposition

    A record for each unit sets out what was found, what it means and whether the unit can stay in service. We review it with your site contact.

  5. 05

    Corrective work is a separate decision

    If something needs fixing, you authorise that separately. We identify whether LiftRight, a partner or your own contractor performs it, and the inspection findings stay separate from any repair quotation.

  6. 06

    Reinspection and final record

    Required reinspection or retesting and acceptance follow the corrective work. The final record documents the outcome and any next actions.

What the record is not

  • It is not a guarantee of future safe operation. Condition changes with use, and pre-use checks remain the operator's responsibility.
  • It is not a repair quotation. Corrective work is scoped and authorised separately.
  • It is not a substitute for required engineering review, specialist testing or manufacturer requirements where those apply.
  • Photographs support the inspection. They do not replace it.

Common questions

How quickly will I get the records?

We agree the delivery timing with you when we scope the work and confirm it on the day.

Can you send records to my customer or head office?

Yes, with your authorisation. Tell us who should receive what.

Do you keep a copy?

We retain inspection records so they can be referenced at the next inspection and support traceability if a question is raised later. Records are not published or shared without your authorisation.

What if I disagree with a finding?

Tell us. Findings are documented with photographs so they can be reviewed, and we will discuss them with you.

Ready to organise your inspections?

Tell us about your equipment and site. We confirm scope and come back with a quote.
